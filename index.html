WHAT IS “RENT”

RENT is a rock musical by Jonathan Larson about a year in the life of a group of young artists in New York in the early nineties. They can’t pay the rent, they fall in love, they lose each other, and they’re right in the middle of the AIDS crisis.

Larson wrote it about his own friends and neighbourhood. He died suddenly, at age 35, the night before the first preview of the show, and never saw what came next: RENT moved to Broadway, won the Pulitzer Prize and four Tony Awards (including Best Musical), ran for twelve years and became one of the most iconic musicals ever written.

People get sick, fall in and out of love, behave like complete assholes — often all in the same song. Somehow, you end up rooting for them. Larson took Puccini’s La Bohème and moved it to 1990s New York.

Thirty years later, we’re bringing his New York to Belgrade, and it feels just as relevant here and now.

OUR “RENT”

People who saw RENT when it first opened in New York in 1996 described it as watching your friends walk onto a stage and sing about the life you were all living. Over the years it has been polished into a beautiful, flawless Broadway production.

We wanted to bring that first feeling back.

We cast people who recognise their own lives in this story, and we let them be themselves on stage. Every night they pour their hearts out, and the show comes out honest in a way you can’t rehearse. That’s exactly how we like it.

A ticket costs 2,000 dinars (about 20 US dollars), the same price RENT charged for its famous front-row seats on Broadway in 1996.

EVERYTHING IS RENT

The small print in your contract doesn’t mention that everything is rent. The flat, the city, the people you call family on a Tuesday night. We take as much as our hearts, our health and our bank cards can manage, and we keep it until our hearts expire (or our visas do).

For the characters, the crisis was AIDS. Today, everyone in the room has a crisis of their own. For some of us, it came with a war and a packed suitcase. For others, it’s the slow realisation that the place you call home doesn’t feel like yours anymore. It often feels as though there’s nothing we can do. Trust people in power who haven’t listened to us in years? Work ourselves to death just to keep a roof over our heads on a burning planet?

RENT is about living right through it and not lowering your voice along the way.

We are also here to remind you there is still love. There is still friendship. There is still sex (safe, please!). No war, no border and no breaking news can take that away, and we’re angry that anyone tried.

TEAM

We’re a cast and crew from Serbia, Russia and the US who decided to stage a Broadway musical with whatever time and money we could offer. We did it because we love this show deeply, and because right now Belgrade needs this story more than ever.

WHAT TO EXPECT

During the first act, you’ll be standing, so feel free to dance and move around like you’re at a gig.

For the second act, you’ll have a seat, just like in a regular theatre. The bar is open before the show and during intermission.

You’ll be much closer to the stage than usual, close enough to see everything, and the actors won’t pretend you’re invisible. We’d love for you to be part of the show, and we’ll do our best to make you feel like you’re right inside the story.

You won’t get anything extra. Same show, no special seats. Your support will help us pay for equipment and venue rental, put on more performances of RENT in Belgrade, and hopefully even pay our cast and crew a little something.
